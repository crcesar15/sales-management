## Executive Summary

Repair the backend financial core. Consolidate FIFO logic into `FifoStockDeductionService`, centralize cash-register shift transitions in `TRANSITION_MAP`, fix `recalculateStock()` call sites, and configure daily log rotation for `browser.log`. This plan touches only services, models, config, and the shift enum. Controller and resource fixes are deferred to later plans.
